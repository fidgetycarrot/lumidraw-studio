# Lumi Studio 1.10.3: remove or delete pictures from the chat

Before this, Remove and Delete only existed on the small pictures in the Studio tab's history list. Tapping a picture in the chat opened the viewer, which had no way to get rid of it.

## What changed

- **The viewer has two new buttons.** Tap a picture in the chat and you'll see them at the bottom:
  - **Remove from chat** takes the picture out of that message. It stays in your history and library, so you can add it back later.
  - **Delete** removes it everywhere: the chat, your history and the image library. It asks first: the button turns red and says "Tap again to delete". If you don't tap again within 4 seconds, it resets.
- **Older pictures can be cleaned up too.** History keeps only the newest 80 pictures. Before, tapping an older Lumi Studio picture in the chat did nothing at all. Now it opens the viewer with only Remove from chat and Delete, because there are no saved settings left to edit.
- Other pictures in the chat, such as avatars, are not touched.
