# Lumi Studio 1.13.3: armor stays on, and aim at the right thing

Report: "Vesper is shooting Grimnir for some reason? Also, Grimnir should be in his space marine 40K armor."

- **Body tags the clothes cover are left out.** Grimnir's sheet has "large pectorals, abs, chest hair, navel hair". With those in the prompt, Anima showed the skin and dropped the armor down to a belt. Now, when someone wears something over their chest (a shirt, coat, armor, robe, corset, dress and so on), tags for bare chest, stomach and back are left out of that picture. That covers abs, pectorals, nipples, chest and navel hair, back and stomach tattoos, and "along spine" details. Full-body armor (power armor, plate, space suits) also hides arm tags like biceps and shoulder tattoos. Nothing is left out when the outfit is open, a harness, a crop top, or anything else that shows skin. Picture details lists what was left out.
- **Aiming needs a target.** The parser is now told to name what someone aims, points, shoots or throws at, for example "aiming at drop pod". With nothing said, Anima points the gun at the only other person in the picture.

Checked on ten earlier reports: the only other change is Blark losing "large pectorals" under his iron breastplate.
