# Lumi Studio 1.18.4 — stowed helmets; full reports for pasted pictures

- **Double helmet fixed (MOSS):** a helmet that is carried or stowed ("helmet on belt", "mag-locked at his hip", "under arm", "on the desk", "held", "hanging"…) no longer counts as worn. Before, "helmet on belt" also produced "His helmet is on and covers his whole head", and MOSS drew one on his head and one on his belt. Now the prompt says "His head is bare and his face is visible; the helmet is not on his head", and his face, hair and eyes are described again.
- **Reports for Prompt only pictures:** a pasted picture now keeps the parser's reply and the full compile notes, so its report is as useful as any other picture's (the first pasted report had no parser reply).
