# Lumi Studio 1.39.0

**Mark pictures without leaving the scene.**

- Every chat picture has 👍 and 👎 in its bottom-right corner. They show when you point at the picture and stay hidden otherwise. A marked picture keeps its one button faintly visible.
- 👎 shows a row of reasons for a few seconds: Clothes, Pose, Face, Background, Wrong people, Other. Tap any, or ignore them and they go away.
- Tap the same button again to take the mark off.
- At the moment you mark, LS saves the full report (prompt, parser reply, story text, which model read it) and a copy of the picture. A later swipe or edit can't change it.
- **Settings → Marked** lists everything marked. **Save all as one file** makes one HTML file with every picture, its reasons, its prompt and its full report. **Clear all** (tap twice) empties the list; × removes one.
- Keeps the last 60 marks.
