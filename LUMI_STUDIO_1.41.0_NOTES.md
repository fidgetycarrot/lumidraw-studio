# Lumi Studio 1.41.0

**Attitude on character sheets.**

- New field in the character and persona editor (MOSS & Outfit tab): **Attitude (how they come across)**. A few plain words, like "teasing, confident, sultry".
- The parser sees it as a light guide. When the story leaves someone's posture and face open, it shows the attitude in one or two small cues: how they stand or sit, plus one face cue. It varies them from picture to picture.
- The moment always wins: fear, pain, grief, surprise, sleep, a struggle, or anything the story says about their face or body replaces it. No winking, pin-up poses or posing for the camera unless the story has it.
- Never pasted into the prompt, so what you see in the prompt is still only what the parser chose.
- LumiCast can send and receive it: the shared sheet carries `attitude` (up to 200 characters), both ways.
