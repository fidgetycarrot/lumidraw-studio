# Lumi Studio 1.42.0

- **End of prompt** box on the Story tab, in Style & negative prompt, next to Prompt prefix. Whatever is in it goes at the very end of every story picture's prompt, after the last person's line, on its own line. It shows in the prompt like everything else, and the picture's notes say it was added.
- Studio still sends exactly what you type, so add it there by hand when you want it.
