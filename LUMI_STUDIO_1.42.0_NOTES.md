# Lumi Studio 1.42.0

- **End of prompt** box on the Story tab, in Style & negative prompt, next to Prompt prefix. Whatever is in it goes at the very end of every story picture's prompt, after the last person's line, on its own line. It shows in the prompt like everything else, and the picture's notes say it was added.
- Studio still sends exactly what you type, so add it there by hand when you want it.

## 1.42.1
From the Reverie's new cedar-and-iron gym-bedroom, which came out as a cramped wooden box with a folio and key in front.
- **Things are props in pictures of people.** Their first two look tags now go after the room, in room of their own, instead of first. Before, seven tags for a folio and a key went first and pushed the steam and the bed out of the prompt. With nobody in the picture, a thing still goes first with its full look.
- **All of a place's saved look fits.** Room for all six place tags next to the moment's own (the light, the steam).
- **Size and layout.** The parser is asked to start a place's look with its size or shape when the story gives it ("spacious room", "high ceiling", "long narrow room").
- **First picture in a newly described place is framed wide** (full body or wide shot), so the room the story just described shows.
