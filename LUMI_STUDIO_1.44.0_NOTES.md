# Lumi Studio 1.44.0

Library, step one: portraits and the sheet check-up.

## Portraits
- Every character and persona card shows a face. A card without one shows the first letter and a **Make** button; with one, point at it for **New**. Click a portrait to see it bigger.
- Portraits are drawn straight from the sheet, no model call: the portrait style (or your story style), "upper body, portrait, simple background", and the character's own tags, through the same prompt builder story pictures use (so a femboy still gets his femboy line, weights convert, and so on).
- Same seed every time for a character, so a sheet's before and after compare fairly.
- On NovelAI they're 832×1216, a size Opus makes for free.
- **Make missing portraits** (Characters and Personas toolbars) draws everyone without one, one at a time in the background. Archived entries are skipped.
- New characters and personas get a portrait as soon as they're saved, including ones from LumiCast or Paige. **Settings → Image apps → Library portraits** has the switch to turn that off, and a **Portrait style** box (empty = story style).
- Portraits never go into picture history or a chat.
- NovelAI now draws one picture at a time across all of Lumi Studio, so background portraits and story pictures don't collide.

## Sheet check-up
- Cards show a yellow "N tips" badge; point at it for the list. Opening a sheet shows the check-up at the top with its portrait and a **New portrait from the saved sheet** button. Tips update as you type.
- Tips: too many body tags (more than five), tags that pull against each other (slim / narrow waist with curvy / wide hips / big sizes), back tags that turn people around, plain "large breasts", things said twice ("glasses" and "thin metal-framed reading glasses"), long made-up phrases, few face and hair details. "No Attitude line" shows in the sheet but doesn't count toward the badge.
- It never changes a sheet.
