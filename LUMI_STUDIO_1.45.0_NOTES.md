# Lumi Studio 1.45.0

Library, step two: stories, tidy-up, archive, and presets by app.

## Stories
- Characters and Personas get a row of story chips above the list: **All**, one chip per story, **Not in a story**, and **Archived**. A story is a character card in Lumiverse; every chat with that card counts. Who's in it comes from those chats' casts, the persona each chat is played as, and characters a story made there. Most recent story first. Your pick is remembered.
- Chats that no longer exist don't make a story.

## Archive
- Every character, persona and preset card has **Archive** (or **Bring back** under Archived). Archived entries are hidden from the Library lists, never deleted, and still work wherever they're linked. The preset in use can't be archived.
- Presets get their own **In use and ready / Archived** chips once something is archived.

## Tidy up
- A **Tidy up** button on Characters, Personas and Presets lists suggestions, each with an Archive button (and "Archive all" per group). Nothing changes until you press one.
  - **Likely duplicates:** same story name, same library name, or one name starting with the other ("Brooke Rivers" / "Brooke Rivers Anima"). Never suggests one a story uses; two that are both in stories are treated as different people who share a name.
  - **Not in any story:** and not edited in a month.
  - **Presets for apps you're not using:** a different app from the preset in use, not edited in a month.

## Presets by app
- The preset list is grouped by app (NovelAI, Draw Things, SwarmUI…). The app of the preset in use comes first and opens; the others fold up. Click a group to open or fold it; that's remembered. Everything opens while you search.

## 1.45.1
- Characters a story makes get a portrait automatically too (when the Settings switch is on), not only ones saved by hand, from LumiCast or from Paige.
