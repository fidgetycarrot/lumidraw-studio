# Lumi Studio 1.46.0

Library, step three: faces by story.

## The wall
- Characters and Personas are now rows of portraits, one row per story (a story is a character card in Lumiverse). The story played most recently comes first, with who you played as and when.
- Hover a face for **Open**, **New face** (or **Make face**), **Not here**, and **Archive**. Click a face to open the sheet. A yellow dot counts sheet tips.
- A slim bar on top: **Find a character…** searches names and sheets and shows the matches as one grid. The **⋯** menu has Make missing portraits, Tidy up, and Look for stories again.
- Stories not played in a month fold into **Older stories**. The 📌 on a story keeps it at the top.
- **Not in a story** and **Archived** sit at the bottom, folded. Archived faces offer Bring back and Delete.

## Filing people
- **Drag** a face onto a story to add them, or onto "Not in a story" to take them out. A character added to a story joins the cast of that story's chats, so they're drawn when they show up; taking them out removes them again.
- The **＋** at the end of each row opens a picker with search.
- **Name check:** a new character with no story gets looked up in your recent chats. If the name turns up in exactly one story, they're filed there with a small **?** (use Not here if it's wrong). **Find their stories** under "Not in a story" runs the same check for everyone there. One-word names need to show up at least twice.
- **From Paige and LumiCast:** a sheet can now say which card it's for and whether the character is a lead, a regular or a minor part. Those characters file themselves, leads first. On the first chat with that card, the cast starts with them, and a single linked persona is used for that chat. Your own drag wins over a later LumiCast update that repeats the same story.

## Notes
- The story sort dropdown, the story chips and "Hide ones a story made" are gone for Characters and Personas; the wall replaces them. Presets are unchanged.
